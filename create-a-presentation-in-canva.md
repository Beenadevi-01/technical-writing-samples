# Create a presentation in Canva

Create a presentation in Canva by choosing a template, adding your content, and presenting or sharing it.

## Before you begin

You need a Canva account. If you do not have one, see [Sign up for Canva](sign-up-for-canva.md).

## Steps

1. Sign in to Canva at **Canva.com**.
2. On the home page, click **Create a design**.
3. Select **Presentation**.
4. In the side panel, browse the templates and click one to apply it to your slide.
5. Click a text box and replace the placeholder text with your own content.
6. To add images, shapes, or icons, click **Elements** in the side panel, search for an item, and click it to add it to the slide.
7. To add another slide, click **Add page** below the current slide.
8. When you finish, click **Present** and select a presentation mode.

Canva saves your work automatically.

## Share or download your presentation

1. Click **Share** in the top-right corner.
2. Choose how you want to share it, or click **Download** and select a file type, such as **PDF** or **PPTX**.

Your presentation is now ready to share.
