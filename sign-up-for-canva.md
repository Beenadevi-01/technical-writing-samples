# Sign up for Canva

Create a free Canva account to start designing and organizing your work.

## Before you begin

You need an email address or a Google account.

## Steps

1. Open a web browser and go to **Canva.com**.
2. Click **Sign up**.
3. Enter your email address, or choose **Continue with Google**.
4. Follow the on-screen instructions to finish creating your account.

You are now signed in to Canva.

## If you already have an account

1. Go to **Canva.com** and click **Log in**.
2. Enter your email address, or choose **Continue with Google**.

You are now signed in to Canva.
